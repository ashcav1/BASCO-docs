basco.ad.mascon

Exact mutual potential of two point-mass bodies; no series truncation.

U = -G M_A M_B sum_ij w_i w_j / |r + C_AB q_j - p_i|

with p_i, q_j the fixed body-frame positions of the primary's and secondary's point masses, w their normalized mass fractions, and M_A, M_B read from the moment cubes. A mass solve-for scales a body at fixed normalized moments (Parameters' mass coordinate), which is exactly a uniform scaling of these point masses, so mass derivatives are complete. A single moment cannot change while the points stay fixed, so moment and harmonic solve-fors are refused.

MasconGravity

Import as basco.ad.MasconGravity

MasconGravity(primary: MasconSet, secondary: MasconSet)

Retained exact point-mass AD gravity extension/reference utility. It lies outside the maintained Hou-first development scope and does not imply native analytic support.

Point-mass gravity for two MasconSet bodies, used with ADTransport.

Build each Body from the same set, Body(name, mascons.distribution(order)), so the moments that drive rotation and mass ratios are those of the points; check_model enforces that agreement before propagation.
