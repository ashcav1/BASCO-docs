basco.plotting.slide_deck

A multi-page 16:9 PDF of figures and short text slides, for presenting a study.

Pages are ordinary Matplotlib figures, so any slide-sized figure can be added. Title, text and table slides share the figures' typography. Collections inside figures should be rasterized to keep the PDF small; text stays vector.

The theme's settings stay active for the whole life of the PDF. Fonts are embedded when it closes, so closing under different settings from those the pages were drawn with corrupts ligature glyphs (such as "fl" and "ff") in TrueType text.

Import as basco.particles.SlideDeck

SlideDeck(path, *, title, subject='', theme=None)

A PDF of 16:9 pages, written one page at a time.

with SlideDeck("study.pdf", title="My study") as deck:
    deck.title_slide("My study", "what it is about")
    deck.add(figure)                       # any Matplotlib figure of slide size
    deck.text_slide("Findings", ["first point", "second point"])
