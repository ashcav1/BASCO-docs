basco.covariance.kaula

Kaula rules: a priori uncertainty of gravity coefficients from the roughness of a shape.

A Kaula rule says that the normalized gravity coefficients of degree l have a typical size

sigma_l = K / l^alpha

BASCO fits K and alpha for a body from an ensemble of perturbed copies of its shape, the method of the Hera paper:

  1. Make many rough copies of the shape (RadialShapeSampler), and optionally copies with a non-uniform density (DensityVariation). Compute the normalized coefficients of each through degree 12 (build_shape_ensemble).
  2. For each degree take the size of the coefficients: the absolute value of the zonal one (order 0), and the RMS of all the others. These are the sizes of the coefficients themselves; the nominal shape's coefficients are not subtracted.
  3. Take the RMS of those sizes over the rough shapes and fit a straight line to log(size) against log(l), separately for the zonal and the non-zonal coefficients. If the fitted exponent agrees with 2 within its 1-sigma, exponent 2 is used (Kaula's value).
  4. Raise K until the rule lies above every member of the ensemble at every degree: the rule is an upper bound, not a best fit.

KaulaRule is the fitted rule of one body. KaulaPrior turns the rules of the two bodies into a priori information for a covariance analysis.

build_shape_ensemble(mesh, sampler, *, reference_radius_m, body, max_degree=12, density_models=(), realign=False)

Generate coefficient statistics from shape and optional density members, independently of dynamical order. realign=True aligns each perturbed copy; False retains its perturbation-frame convention. These are scientifically different ensembles.

The CoefficientEnsemble of a shape: the nominal mesh, every rough copy from sampler and one member per entry of density_models.

Degree 12 on a fine mesh takes a while. realign=False keeps each perturbed copy in the frame of the nominal shape, as the paper did; True moves each to its own centre of mass and principal axes first.

KaulaPrior

Import as basco.covariance.KaulaPrior

KaulaPrior(parameters: object, body_rules: dict, inertia_integral_priors: bool = True, name: str = 'Shape-derived Kaula prior')

Map the rule normalization/radius through the physical-coordinate Jacobian. inertia_integral_priors=True additionally selects the paper's independent RMS-touching raw-moment constraints; it is separate from harmonic knowledge and adds no implicit mass/state/barycenter prior.

A priori information for a covariance analysis from the Kaula rules of the bodies.

parameters: the ParameterSet of the analysis. body_rules: {"primary": KaulaRule, "secondary": KaulaRule} (or "A" / "B"); a body may be left out. inertia_integral_priors: also give each estimated inertia integral its own a priori sigma (the paper's Eq. 13): the RMS of the sigmas of the harmonics that the integral contributes to, times R^degree. This is a separate assumption on top of the rule for the harmonics. name: identifies this information.

The rule constrains the harmonics of each body, each with its own sigma and no correlation. Masses, the initial state and biases get no a priori information from it.
