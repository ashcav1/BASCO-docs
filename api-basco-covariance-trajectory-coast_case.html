basco.covariance.trajectory.coast_case

A worked case: the covariance of a particle coasting kilometres from the binary.

Three coast arcs past Didymos and Dimorphos, a few kilometres away and a few hours long (they are not the trajectory of any mission). The Hou mutual potential drives the binary and the exterior fields of both bodies drive the particle. No solar radiation pressure, solar tide or manoeuvre. The initial covariance is declared; nothing is observed or estimated.

problem = make_problem("binary_flyby")     # model, initial state, initial covariance
trajectory = run_problem(problem)          # trajectory with its covariance
print(summary(problem, trajectory))

examples/covariance/trajectory/long_trajectories.py runs it. The code here was that example before 2026-11-11; it is kept as it was written, and the tests use it.
