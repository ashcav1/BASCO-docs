basco.square_root

Square-root statistics assembly shared by single- and multi-arc linearizations.

Whitened data rows and independent prior blocks form one square-root system; the numerical solver alone decides rank and covariance. Nothing here knows the binary, its observations or its arcs.

SquareRootSystem

SquareRootSystem(data_rows: object, prior_rows: object, data_rhs: object, prior_rhs: object, residual: object, noise: object, blocks: tuple)

One weighted least-squares/MAP system: whitened rows and residuals.

data_rows/data_rhs whiten the design rows and the observed-minus-predicted residual; prior_rows/prior_rhs are the stacked prior blocks and their mean-minus-reference residuals. residual is the unwhitened data residual.
