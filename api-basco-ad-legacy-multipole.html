basco.ad.legacy.multipole

Deprecated Cartesian multipole compatibility. Hou AD is the maintained default; old imports remain available.

Cartesian multipole mutual potential of any order 2-6, written without Hou's tables.

With r the secondary's center of mass relative to the primary's (frame A), rho_A a primary mass element and rho_B' = C_AB rho_B a secondary element,

U = -G sum_{A,B} dm_A dm_B / |r + rho_B' - rho_A|.

Taylor-expanding 1/|r + s| about r, s = rho_B' - rho_A, and expanding s^gamma binomially gives

U = -G sum_{|alpha| + |beta| <= N} (-1)^|alpha| binom(alpha + beta, alpha)
        b_{alpha + beta}(r) T_A^alpha T_B'^beta,

with multi-index moments T^alpha = int rho^alpha dm, b_gamma = d^gamma(1/|r|)/gamma! and T_B' the secondary's moments in frame A. The total moment degree N is the same truncation as Hou's total mutual order, so orders 2, 4 and 6 must agree with the Hou series to rounding; odd orders 3 and 5 are new truncations.

Independent ingredients: - b_gamma from the Cartesian recurrence for the Taylor coefficients of the Coulomb potential (Duan and Krasny, J. Comput. Chem. 22, 2001, rewritten for derivatives with respect to r): n r^2 b_a + (2n-1) sum_i r_i b_{a-e_i} + (n-1) sum_i b_{a-2e_i} = 0, n = |a|; checked against nested AD of 1/|r|; - T_B' by rotating each full symmetric moment tensor with C_AB on every index.

CartesianMultipoleGravity

Import as basco.ad.CartesianMultipoleGravity, basco.ad.multipole.CartesianMultipoleGravity

CartesianMultipoleGravity(mutual_order: int = 2)

Deprecated compatibility implementation, retained for historical callers/reference checks. HouGravity is the maintained gravity path; this class is not the recommended basis for new BASCO studies.

Truncated mutual expansion through total moment degree mutual_order (2-6).

SI potential(r, C_AB, T_A, T_B, G) as in basco.ad.SIPotential's contract; every moment through mutual_order enters, so all mass, moment and harmonic solve-fors are supported. Orders 2, 4 and 6 equal the Hou series to rounding; 3 and 5 add the odd-degree couplings Hou's tables cannot run separately. Higher orders exceed the moment tables and compile slowly.
