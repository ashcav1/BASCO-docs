basco.tumbling

Nominal Hou trajectories and data-only secondary-attitude diagnostics.

This module never requests variational columns, observations or a covariance solve. Native30 remains the scientific record; any rotation projection below is confined to explicitly reported attitude postprocessing.

propagate_nominal

propagate_nominal(model, duration, dt=10.0, cadence=60.0, *, dcm_tolerance=1e-05)

Nominal-only coupled Hou propagation through the retained C++ RK4 value integrator. It requests no STM, parameter sensitivities, observations, priors or covariance solve. Duration/output cadence and cadence/step must be compatible integer grids. Native30 states retain their original units/rotation drift; output domain checks are sampled checks, not continuous contact detection.

Propagate Hou nominal30 through the retained value-only C++ RK4 runner.

Plain duration/dt/cadence are elapsed seconds; Astropy times are accepted. dt must divide cadence and cadence must divide duration. Both endpoints are returned. No propagated DCM is normalized or projected. The retained problem/config/run adapter builds nominal moments with zero derivative columns; no variational integrator or estimator is called. Domain checks cover output samples and bounding spheres, not intervening collisions.

attitude_history

attitude_history(history, *, sequence='XYZ', unwrap=True, window=None, gimbal_tolerance=1e-06, dcm_tolerance=1e-05, project_rotations=True, max_angle_step=np.pi / 2)

Data-only Hill-relative attitude diagnostics. Choose intrinsic XYZ (123) or ZYX (321), with output columns always roll/pitch/yaw. Local unwrapping splits at singular/unresolved chart changes. Excursions are sampled half-ranges, not fitted periodic amplitudes or dynamical classifications. Body directions remain useful when Euler angles are unavailable.

Report physical Hill-relative Euler angles, spin and nonsingular axes.

Hill x is outward secondary-minus-primary r_N/|r_N|, z is the normalized inertial r_N cross v_N, and y=z cross x; velocity is the inertial velocity resolved in A, not dr_A/dt. C_NB=C_NA@C_AB and C_HB=C_NH.T@C_NB.

sequence='XYZ' means intrinsic 1-2-3, C_HB=Rx(roll)@Ry(pitch)@Rz(yaw). 'ZYX' means intrinsic 3-2-1, C_HB=Rz(yaw)@Ry(pitch)@Rx(roll). Both return roll,pitch,yaw columns. SciPy's uppercase convention and gimbal ranges: https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.spatial.transform.Rotation.as_euler.html (retrieved 2026-11-06). Wrapped roll/yaw lie in [-pi,pi], pitch in [-pi/2,pi/2].

By default, the closest SO(3) to raw C_NB is used for attitude diagnostics only; its correction and the raw DCM drift are reported. Native states are untouched. project_rotations=False rejects drift above 1e-12 to avoid silently relying on SciPy's internal matrix orthogonalization.

Gimbal samples remain in angles_rad with SciPy's arbitrary zero third sequence angle; euler_valid marks all three unusable for coordinate ranges. Local roll/yaw unwrapping never crosses these gaps or steps exceeding max_angle_step in either principal roll/yaw coordinate (shortest arc). Such splits also catch sampled Euler branch jumps around a missed gimbal crossing. Cadence must still resolve the motion: no samples can detect a hidden full turn. window=(start_s,stop_s) selects samples for excursions only, inclusively; there is no interpolation or period inference.
