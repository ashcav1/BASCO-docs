basco.covariance.hera.analysis

Build a covariance analysis set up like the Hera paper.

analysis = paper_analysis("AC2")                       one of the paper's four cases
analysis = make_analysis(HERA_PAPER_DATA, model=...)   the same setup for your own binary

The setup: the relative position, the attitude of both bodies and the position of Didymos about the barycentre are observed every 15 minutes; both masses, the initial state, the position and velocity of the barycentre and the gravity of both bodies are estimated; the a priori information is the paper's Kaula rule.

hera_like_binary

hera_like_binary(*, primary_mass_kg=hera.MASS_A_KG, secondary_mass_kg=hera.MASS_B_KG, secondary_semi_axes_m=(96.0, 74.0, 59.0), secondary_to_primary_euler_xyz_deg=(0.0, 3.0, 15.0), secondary_spin_period_h=11.921493, secondary_spin_tilt_deg=30.0, position_m=None, velocity_m_s=None, G=G_SI, gravity_degree=6)

A binary like Didymos with your own masses, secondary shape and secondary rotation.

The primary keeps the shape of the paper's Didymos; its inertia integrals are scaled to the new mass. The secondary is a uniform ellipsoid with the given semi-axes. Its orientation is an extrinsic x, then y, then z rotation from secondary to primary axes; its spin vector is tilted by secondary_spin_tilt_deg from its own +z axis. The relative position and velocity are those of case AC2 unless given (secondary minus primary, in primary axes). Changing the masses does not recalibrate that orbit or its period.

make_analysis

make_analysis(archive_root, state=None, *, model=None, gravity_degree=6, estimate_degrees=(2, 3, 4), parameterization='raw', higher_degree_bodies='both', prior='kaula', dt=75.0, duration=hera.DURATION_S, partials=None)

Installed, unpropagated Hera recipe. Supply the BASCO-owned reference directory and AC0-AC3, or a complete custom Hou BinaryModel through model=. These inputs are mutually exclusive. Defaults preserve the full paper setup. Custom geometry does not refit paper Kaula rules; choose a prior explicitly for a new scientific model. See the Hera customization guide for executable scripts and frame conventions.

A CovarianceAnalysis set up like the paper. Nothing is propagated here.

archive_root: the folder data/hera_paper (HERA_PAPER_DATA). state: "AC0", "AC1", "AC2" or "AC3"; AC3 when neither state nor model is given. model: your own BinaryModel instead of a paper case. Give state or model, not both. Its gravity order must equal gravity_degree. gravity_degree: order of the mutual gravity: 2, 4 or 6. estimate_degrees: the degrees (from 2, 3, 4) whose gravity parameters are estimated. parameterization: "raw" estimates normalized inertia integrals; "harmonics" estimates harmonics above degree 2. higher_degree_bodies: "both", "primary" or "secondary": whose degrees above 2 are estimated. prior: "kaula" for the paper's Kaula rule with its extra sigmas on the inertia integrals; "none" or None for no a priori information; or a list of your own a priori objects. For your own model, "kaula" still uses the paper's rule, which was fitted to the paper's shapes. dt: RK4 step [s]; the paper uses 75. duration: [s]; the paper's 16 orbits by default. partials: "ad" (default) or "analytic".

Observations are every 900 s, the barycentre has a free offset and rate, and the solver is the PartitionedSquareRootSolver. To change the observations, build a CovarianceAnalysis yourself; this function is short enough to copy from.
