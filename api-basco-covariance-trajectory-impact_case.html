basco.covariance.trajectory.impact_case

A worked case: the covariance of a trajectory to impact on Didymos or Dimorphos.

The shape models in data/shapes define the surface and the binary's inertia integrals. Near the impact site the gravity is a local cylindrical-harmonic field, fitted to the gravity of the uniform polyhedron. Its uncertainty is declared, together with the masses and the particle's initial state: this is not a Hera estimate and not a measured surface field.

problem = make_problem("dimorphos")        # model, local field, initial covariance
trajectory = run_problem(problem)          # trajectory to impact with its covariance
print(trajectory.impact.report.summary())  # impact time and point with 1-sigma

examples/covariance/trajectory/impact_covariance.py runs it. The code here was that example before 2026-11-11; it is kept as it was written, and the tests use it.
