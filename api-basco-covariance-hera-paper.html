basco.covariance.hera.paper

The Hera covariance paper: its exact setup, its archived rows and the comparison with its Table 2.

There are two ways to get the paper's numbers.

  • Fresh: propagate with BASCO. paper_analysis (analysis.py) does this, with hera_parameters and hera_priors from this file.
  • Replay: load_hera_archive loads the design-matrix rows that the paper's own runs saved and analyses them with BASCO's solvers. Nothing is propagated.

The archived rows are in the units and unknowns of the original code: kilometres, the masses as GM, and the barycentre as two Legendre coefficients per axis. load_hera_archive converts them to BASCO's unknowns in SI units.

How the paper reports its numbers (hera_report, table2_percent):

  • A priori: the Kaula rule on the harmonics and, in addition, an independent sigma on every estimated inertia integral (the paper's Eq. 13).
  • The harmonics are fully normalized.
  • The moment of inertia is given as Izz / (M R^2), which does not change when only the mass changes.

Where the numbers come from: Table 2 of the paper (version of 2026-10-16) was made from its "round 20" runs, four single-arc cases named raw34ABm. "Round 21" holds sensitivity studies, not replacements. Both are in data/hera_paper with checksums.

prepare_hera_moments

Import as basco.covariance.hera.prepare_hera_moments

prepare_hera_moments(mesh_path)

The inertia integrals through degree 6 of a shape model, in the form build_hera_model reads.

The OBJ file is taken to be in kilometres. The mesh is moved to its centre of mass and principal axes, with the axis directions chosen so that T300 and T030 are not negative. The integrals of degree 1 and the products of inertia, zero up to rounding, are set to exactly zero, and their size before that is recorded. Returns a dictionary that can be saved as JSON; nothing is written here.
