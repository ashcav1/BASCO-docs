basco.particles.initial_conditions

Starting positions and velocities of many particles, as a table.

One row per particle. Build a table from arrays, or read it from a CSV file:

start = InitialConditions(position, velocity)          # two (N, 3) arrays, m and m/s
start = read_initial_conditions("my_particles.csv")

The table does not say which frame its numbers are in. You say that when you propagate: at a surface site (x east, y north, z up, velocity relative to the ground), in a body's frame, or in the inertial frame.

CSV layout: a header line, then one line per particle. The columns x_m, y_m, z_m, vx_m_s, vy_m_s and vz_m_s are required. A "name" column is optional. Any other column is kept as an extra value per particle (for example the impact speed of the drop that produced it).
