basco.binary.rotating_frame

The binary's rotating frame.

x points from the primary's centre to the secondary's centre, z along the angular momentum of their relative orbit, and y = z cross x. The origin is the binary's barycentre. Both bodies stay nearly still in this frame, which makes it the natural one for drawing paths that go from one body to the other.

RotatingFrame

RotatingFrame(times, binary_states, masses)

The rotating frame at a list of saved times.

times: (T,) seconds. axes: (T, 3, 3); the columns are the frame's x, y and z in inertial axes. centres: (T, 2, 3) body centres in the rotating frame [m]. body_rotations: (T, 2, 3, 3) body-frame to rotating-frame rotations, for drawing shapes.
