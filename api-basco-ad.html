basco.ad

Optional automatic-differentiation (AD) backend, kept as a deletable copy.

The native/manual analytic backend remains available explicitly and is not modified. This package holds a separate, function-by-function copy of the retained Hou/GUBAS gravity and equations of motion (native/legacy/gubas_core.cpp), re-expressed in JAX. Forces, torques and the variational Jacobians A = dF/dx and B = dF/dT all come from automatic differentiation of that copy; no hand-derived partial is called. Deleting this package (and tests/test_ad_*.py) leaves the rest of BASCO unchanged.

Select it per study, with the same physical model and observations:

from basco.ad import ADTransport
study = bc.CovarianceStudy(model, parameters, plan, bc.RK4(60.0),
                           transport=ADTransport())

A different gravity model needs only its potential energy (the contract is basco.contracts.GravityModel). Any gravity object with mutual_order and an SI potential(r, C_AB, T_A, T_B, G) method can replace HouGravity in BinaryDynamics; ADTransport differentiates it automatically. Shipped models: SecondDegreeGravity (closed form, order 2), deprecated CartesianMultipoleGravity compatibility under ad.legacy, and MasconGravity (exact point-mass bodies, no truncation). conservation gives the energy and angular momentum of any of them along a propagated trajectory.

Hou AD is the default workflow. Requires the basco[ad] extra (JAX) at propagation. ADTransport construction is lazy; evaluator/potential use enables float64. hou_tables is plain NumPy and needs no JAX.
