basco.particles

Particle simulation around a binary asteroid.

from basco import particles as bp

environment = bp.didymos_environment(state="AC2")
site = environment.site("Dimorphos", latitude_deg=0.0, longitude_deg=170.0)
initial_conditions = bp.read_initial_conditions("data/milani_bed1/dimorphos.csv")
trajectories = bp.propagate(
    environment, initial_conditions, site=site, duration_s=24 * 3600, workers=8
)
bp.plot_trajectories(trajectories.impacting("Dimorphos"), frame="Dimorphos", file="a.png")

Where things are:

initial_conditions.py   the table of initial positions and velocities, and its CSV file
environment.py          the binary, its shape models and the gravity model;
                        didymos_environment()
sites.py                a point on a body's surface with an east-north-up frame
trajectories.py         propagate() and the Trajectories it returns; save and load
plots/                  figures and animations of trajectories
propagator.py           the propagator underneath: one binary and a few particles
                        integrated together, with event detection
dynamics.py             the equations of motion that the propagator integrates
solar.py                optional solar gravity and radiation pressure
state.py                Particle, ParticleOutcome and StateHistory, used by the propagator

A particle is massless. It feels both bodies; it does not move them, and particles do not feel each other.
