basco.near_field.geometry

Declared mass-free cylindrical domains in an asteroid's body frame.

The patch specifies a coordinate domain. It does not inspect a surface mesh or certify that the domain contains no material. That scientific assumption belongs to the caller and must be checked against the body's shape before propagation.

CylindricalPatch

Import as basco.near_field.CylindricalPatch

CylindricalPatch(origin: object, radius: object, height: object, rotation: object = field(default_factory=lambda: np.eye(3)))

Fixed body-frame cylinder with a base origin and local positive-z axis. Geometry uses SI metres and a proper cylinder-to-body rotation. Its boundary is included with a documented roundoff tolerance. The caller must establish that the domain is mass-free; constructing a patch does not check a shape mesh or include uncertain patch pose.

A fixed cylinder whose base is at origin and whose axis is local +z.

Parameters

origin : (3,) array or length Quantity Base-centre location in the asteroid body frame, in metres. radius, height : positive float or length Quantity Cylinder radius and height in metres. The domain includes its boundary. rotation : (3, 3) array, optional Proper orthogonal C_body,cylinder: its columns are the cylinder axes expressed in the body frame. Defaults to aligned axes. No projection or correction of an invalid rotation is performed.

Notes

This is a user-declared mass-free domain, including the exterior limiting surface if appropriate. It is neither a shape model nor a global field. The domain check allows 32 float64 epsilon times the radius at the side/base, and max(radius, height) at the top, solely for transformation roundoff. Evaluated coordinates are not clipped. Geometry is fixed; uncertainty in patch pose or shape is not modeled here.
