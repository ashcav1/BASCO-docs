basco._legacy.f2bptoolkit.kaula

Legacy compatibility reference. Prefer current BASCO workflows; copied implementation details are not new development targets.

Kaula-type a priori for gravity coefficients, built per body from a shape model (the Bennu recipe of Scheeres et al. 2016 / McMahon et al. 2018, the Hera per-degree envelope of Gramigna et al. 2024).

The estimator never imports this module. It produces, and reads back, prior FILES: absolute sigmas on normalised coefficients, per (body, l, m), at the body's volume-equivalent reference radius, with their provenance. cases.run_case consumes them through its generic coefficient_prior input; any other prior can be fed the same way.

Pieces ellipsoid_mesh an icosphere mapped onto a triaxial ellipsoid random_radial_field a band-limited Gaussian field on the sphere, the implementation of "correlation length" perturb_mesh radial displacement of a mesh by such a field density_variants core / shell density models and the equatorial torus density variant, mass held normalised_coefficients (l, m) -> Cbar/Sbar of a truth cube degree_sizes sigma_l, zonal and non-zonal, from coefficients fit_rule K, alpha by least squares in log space build_prior / load_prior the JSON file with everything inside

build_prior

build_prior(body: str, R_m: float, ensemble_coefs, nominal_coefs, density_coefs, factor: float=1.0, degrees=(2, 3, 4), settings: Optional[dict]=None, fit_max_degree: int=12, alpha_lock: float=2.0) -> dict

Assemble a prior file with a McMahon-style adopted power law.

Exactly the McMahon et al. 2018 construction, in the space of the COEFFICIENTS THEMSELVES (no nominal subtraction, their Figs 8/9): per degree, the zonal size is each member's |Cl0| and the non-zonal size the rms of its remaining slots; the envelope is the rms of those sizes over the shape copies. A power law K/l^alpha is fitted through the envelopes over degrees 2..fit_max_degree; if the fitted alpha lies within one standard error of alpha_lock, the exponent is locked there and K refitted. The upper bound raises K until the law clears every member, the density models included. The ADOPTED sigma at each applied degree is the upper-bound law, with no safety factor; factor is recorded but not applied. The prior MEAN remains the uniform-density value.
