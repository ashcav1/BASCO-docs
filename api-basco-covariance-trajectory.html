basco.covariance.trajectory

The covariance of a particle's trajectory.

Given how well the binary is known (its initial state, its masses and its gravity) and how well the particle's own initial state is known, how uncertain is the particle's position later, or the time and place of its impact? No observations of the particle are involved: an existing covariance is propagated along the trajectory.

quantity at time t  =  function of  q
cov(quantity)       =  J cov(q) J^T,        J = d quantity / d q

Here q are the uncertain inputs: the initial state deviation of the binary, the physical parameters, and the particle's initial position and velocity. Their covariance is the InitialCovariance. In this package the word "source" in a name (source_jacobian, initial_source_jacobian, delta_source) always refers to q: a "source Jacobian" is a matrix of partials with respect to q.

point_state.py, body_fixed_initial_state.py    the particle's initial state
initial_covariance.py                          the covariance of q
dynamics.py                                    equations of motion and their partials
variational_equations.py                       the trajectory with d state / d q
epoch_mapping.py                               starting the particle later than the
                                               time q refers to
impact.py, impact_trajectory.py                propagation to an impact; covariance of
                                               the impact time and point
saved_analysis.py                              an InitialCovariance from a saved
                                               covariance analysis
coast_case.py, impact_case.py                  complete worked cases

The particle simulation in basco/particles computes trajectories without partials and is separate from this package.
