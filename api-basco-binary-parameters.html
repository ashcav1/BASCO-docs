basco.binary.parameters

Which parameters are estimated, and how they relate to what the equations of motion use.

parameters = (
    EstimatedParameters(model)
    .estimate_masses()                     # both masses
    .estimate_moments(degrees=(2,))        # inertia integrals of degree 2, over the mass
    .estimate_harmonics(degrees=(3, 4))    # harmonics C_lm and S_lm of degrees 3 and 4
    .estimate_initial_state()              # the 18 numbers of the state deviation
)

A body is described by three kinds of parameters:

mass                 "primary.mass"  [kg]
normalized integral  "primary.T200" = T_200 / mass  [m^2]
harmonic             "primary.C30"  [no unit]: a fixed linear combination of the
                     normalized integrals of its degree, divided by R^degree

The equations of motion use the plain inertia integrals T_lmn of both bodies. The matrix ParameterSet.inertia_integral_jacobian = d T / d parameter connects the two: by the chain rule it turns partials with respect to inertia integrals into partials with respect to the estimated parameters.

The initial state is estimated as a state deviation of 18 numbers (see apply_state_deviation in state.py). state_deviation_jacobian is d(GUBAS state) / d(state deviation).

ParameterSet.inertia_integral_values

inertia_integral_values(self, independent_values)

The inertia integrals of both bodies, {"A": {(l, m, n): T}, "B": ...}, for given values of the parameters. This is the exact relation, not its linearization.

A mass replaces the mass. A normalized integral replaces T / mass. A harmonic changes the normalized integrals of its degree by the smallest change that gives the requested value: delta T = W^T (W W^T)^-1 delta c, with W the matrix from integrals to harmonics. At the end every normalized integral is multiplied by the mass.

EstimatedParameters

Import as basco.covariance.EstimatedParameters

EstimatedParameters(model)

Select independent estimated parameters for one nominal model: masses, moments or higher-degree exterior harmonics, and optionally the 18 physical initial-state errors. Selection order determines column order.

Choose the parameters to estimate.

Each estimate_... call adds parameters and returns the object, so calls can be chained. resolve() then gives the ParameterSet.

Integrals or harmonics? The gravity of a body outside itself depends, at degree l, on 2l + 1 harmonics, while there are (l + 1)(l + 2) / 2 inertia integrals of that degree. The remaining combinations do not change the exterior gravity, so no observation of the motion can determine them. Estimating harmonics holds those combinations at their nominal values. Estimating the integrals themselves leaves them free, and they then show up as directions without information. Degree 2 is the exception: the rotation of a body needs all three diagonal integrals, so degree 2 is always estimated as integrals.

EstimatedParameters.resolve

resolve(self)

Resolve the builder into immutable labels, units, positive scales, nominal coordinates, raw moment slots and the full raw-moment Jacobian. Preserve these named layouts with every array.

The ParameterSet of the chosen parameters: nominal values, scales and the matrix d T / d parameter.

For a mass M: every integral of that body is T = M (T / M), so dT / dM = T / M. For a normalized integral t = T / M: dT / dt = M. For a harmonic c: dT / dc = M times a column of W^T (W W^T)^-1.
