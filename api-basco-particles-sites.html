basco.particles.sites

A site: a point on a body's surface with a local east-north-up (ENU) frame.

The site frame has x pointing east, y north and z up, where "up" is the surface normal averaged over a small patch. Latitude and longitude use the body's own frame: latitude from the x-y plane toward +z (the pole) and longitude from +x toward +y. East is pole cross radial direction; at a pole, where east is undefined, x is the body's +y axis.

Import as basco.particles.Site

Site(name, body, point, up, east)

A surface point and its east / north / up axes, all in the body's frame.

smoothed_normal

smoothed_normal(mesh, point, radius_m)

Area-weighted mean outward normal of the faces within radius_m of a point.

The nearest face always counts, so a small radius still returns its normal. A patch whose normals largely cancel (one that wraps much of the body) is refused.
