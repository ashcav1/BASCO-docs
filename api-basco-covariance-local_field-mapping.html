basco.covariance.local_field.mapping

Fitting local coefficients to samples of a field, with the dependence on the parameters.

The samples are a = a0 + Ja dq, with q the uncertain inputs. The local field at the same points is D b, linear in its coefficients b. The least-squares fit gives

b = D^+ a = b0 + J dq,        J = D^+ Ja

so the fitted coefficients carry the uncertainty of q and no more: a fit adds no information. It also cannot tell what the field is where there were no samples.

CoefficientMap

Import as basco.covariance.local_field.CoefficientMap

CoefficientMap(coefficients: object, jacobian: object, operator: object, source_layout: ParameterLayout, coefficient_layout: ParameterLayout, source_nominal: object, unobservable_directions: object, completion: str, fingerprint: str, diagnostics: dict, metadata: dict)

Construct with fit, then bind to an original covariance/consider result or InitialCovariance. Owns the local affine coefficient map, its physical source Jacobian and numerical target nullspace. An explicit conditional completion is recorded because finite exterior information does not uniquely determine a physical near-surface field.

Fitted coefficients and how they depend on the uncertain inputs: b = b0 + J dq.

coefficients: b0. jacobian: J. operator: D^+, from samples to coefficients. source_layout, source_nominal: the inputs q and their nominal values. coefficient_layout: names of the coefficients. unobservable_directions: combinations of coefficients that the samples do not determine. completion: the assumption, in words, under which the fitted field is used where there were no samples (see fit).

CoefficientMap.fit

fit(cls, design, values, source_jacobian, source_layout, coefficient_layout, *, completion, source_nominal=None, row_scales=None, rcond=None, allow_rank_deficient=False, metadata=None)

Use a scaled SVD to fit nominal coefficients and their source derivative from identical deterministic sample rows. No normal matrix, residual-based prior or independent-data covariance is created. Numerical target rank is distinct from source estimation rank. Deficient target fits reject by default; explicitly accepted null directions remain unresolved in bound reports.

Fit coefficients to sample values.

design: D, the matrix from coefficients to the field at the sample points. values: the sampled field a0. source_jacobian: Ja = d a / d q. completion: a sentence stating the assumption under which the fitted field is used where there were no samples, for example "continuation of the order-6 Hou field". It is required, and it is recorded with the result: a fit cannot justify that use by itself. row_scales: typical size of each sample, to balance the fit. rcond: relative threshold for the rank of D. allow_rank_deficient: accept samples that do not determine every coefficient; the undetermined combinations are then set to zero and reported in unobservable_directions. Otherwise that is an error.

CoefficientMap.bind

bind(self, initial_covariance, *, additional_factor=None, unresolved_scaled=None)

Embed named mapping columns into the complete source layout with exact unit checks and known nominal/model-context validation. Retain original map_factor/derived_from_factor delegation and latent ordering. additional_factor declares independent finite coefficient errors; correlated new errors require a complete joint source through direct(). It does not erase unresolved target directions.

The CoefficientCovariance of the fitted coefficients for an InitialCovariance of q.

additional_factor adds uncertainty of the coefficients that does not come from q. unresolved_scaled declares combinations of coefficients as unknown.
