basco.binary.didymos

The Didymos-Dimorphos binary: masses, shapes' moments and initial states.

The numbers are those of the Hera covariance paper (data/hera_paper). Four initial rotation states of Dimorphos are available:

AC0  equilibrium (circular, planar, synchronous)
AC1  libration only
AC2  limited tumbling
AC3  full tumbling

didymos_binary("AC2") is the short way to get the binary. build_hera_model is the function underneath, which the covariance analyses of the paper also use.

_equilibrium_separation

_equilibrium_separation(cubes, G, period)

The separation [m] at which the secondary, on the primary's x axis, orbits with the given period; also returns the orbit rate [rad/s].

On a circular orbit the attraction equals the centripetal force, so the orbit rate at separation r is sqrt((dU/dr) / (reduced mass * r)), with dU/dr from the full order-6 mutual potential. The separation is adjusted until that rate matches the period, starting from Kepler's third law for two point masses.

build_hera_model

Import as basco.covariance.hera.build_hera_model

build_hera_model(inputs, state='AC3', *, gravity_degree=6)

The Didymos-Dimorphos BinaryModel of the Hera paper in one of its four states.

inputs: the dictionary from load_inputs, or the path of the JSON file. state: "AC0", "AC1", "AC2" or "AC3". gravity_degree: order of the mutual gravity series, 2, 4 or 6.

The bodies always carry their inertia integrals through degree 6, and the initial state is always the paper's, which was tuned with order 6. Choosing a lower gravity_degree therefore changes only the forces and torques; the orbit is not retuned.

AC0 is circular, planar and synchronous. Its speed carries the paper's factor 0.995394. AC1, AC2 and AC3 share an eccentricity of 0.0274, a primary tilted by 1 degree, a secondary tilted by 3 degrees and a secondary spin period of 11.921493 h. They differ in the secondary's initial libration angle and in the tilt of its spin vector from its z axis: (10, 0), (10, 20) and (90, 43.09) degrees.
