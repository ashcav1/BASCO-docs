basco.particles.solar

Forces from the Sun: its gravity on the particles and on the binary, and the pressure of sunlight on the particles.

Everything is in SI units, in the inertial axes whose origin is the binary's barycentre. You supply where the Sun is in those axes: one fixed position for a short run (FixedSun), or a table of positions over time (TabulatedSun).

What is modelled:

  • Solar gravity on a particle, measured relative to the binary's barycentre. The barycentre itself falls toward the Sun, so only the difference matters (the solar tide).
  • The same tide on the binary's relative orbit, and the leading (quadrupole) gravity-gradient torque of the Sun on each body.
  • Radiation pressure on the particles, as for a sphere ("cannonball"): pressure times area over mass times reflectivity, pointing away from the Sun, falling off with distance squared.

Not modelled: radiation pressure on the two bodies, and the Sun's force on a body beyond treating it as a point for translation.

Sources (retrieved 2026-11-09):

  • IERS Technical Note 21, chapter 10, p. 81: radiation pressure 4.560e-6 N/m^2 at 1 au. https://www.iers.org/SharedDocs/Publikationen/EN/IERS/Publications/tn/TechnNote21/tn21_81.pdf
  • NASA report 19860005869, Eq. (27): gravity-gradient torque 3 mu / R^3 (s x I s), with s the unit vector to the Sun in the body's axes. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19860005869/downloads/19860005869.pdf
  • JPL Solar System Dynamics, Astrodynamic Parameters: the Sun's GM (DE440) and the astronomical unit. https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/astro_par.html

gravity_difference

gravity_difference(sun, positions, distances, mu)

Solar gravity at each position minus solar gravity at the barycentre, and the latter.

The Sun is so far away that the two nearly cancel. For positions close to the barycentre the difference is therefore computed from a formula that does the cancellation exactly (with expm1 and log1p) instead of subtracting two nearly equal numbers.

SolarForces(sun, *, mu_sun=MU_SUN, gravity=True, srp=True, perturb_binary=True, binary_torques=True, pressure_1au=PRESSURE_1AU, au_m=AU_M)

Which solar effects are switched on, and the constants they use.

sun: a FixedSun or TabulatedSun (anything with position(epoch_s)). gravity: solar gravity on the particles, relative to the barycentre. perturb_binary: with gravity on, also let the Sun perturb the binary's relative orbit. binary_torques: the Sun's gravity-gradient torque on each body. srp: radiation pressure on the particles. mu_sun, pressure_1au, au_m: the Sun's GM, the pressure at 1 au and the length of 1 au.
