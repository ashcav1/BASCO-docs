basco.covariance.local_field

A local model of a body's gravity near a patch of its surface, and its uncertainty.

The Hou series is not valid inside a body's Brillouin sphere, which is where a particle is shortly before it impacts. This package represents the gravity inside a small cylinder above the surface by another set of functions (cylindrical harmonics) that are valid there.

geometry.py       CylindricalDomain: the cylinder
cylindrical.py    the cylindrical harmonics and a field made of them
residual.py       a point mass plus a cylindrical-harmonic remainder
hou.py            samples of a body's Hou gravity, with partials
mapping.py        fit the local coefficients to such samples
uncertainty.py    the covariance of the local coefficients

Two ways to get the local coefficients and their covariance:

  • From the Hou series: sample it outside the Brillouin sphere and fit the local functions to the samples. The coefficients b then depend on the binary's parameters, b = b0 + J dq, so their covariance follows from that of q (the uncertain inputs; "source" in the names here, as in basco/covariance/trajectory). A fit to samples outside the sphere does not make the result accurate inside it; that remains an assumption.
  • Directly: give the coefficients and their covariance yourself.

Nothing here simulates observations or solves an estimation problem.
