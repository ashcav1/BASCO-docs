basco.mascons

Point-mass (mascon) bodies: exact SI moments of a fixed set of point masses.

A MasconSet is a rigid body made of point masses at fixed body-frame positions. Its raw moments T[l, m, n] = sum m_i x_i^l y_i^m z_i^n are exact, so the same body can drive a truncated moment expansion (Hou or Cartesian multipole) and the exact pairwise mutual potential (basco.ad.MasconGravity). That shared body is what makes truncation studies meaningful.

Plain NumPy; the JAX gravity model that uses these points lives in basco.ad.

MasconSet.uniform_ellipsoid

uniform_ellipsoid(cls, axes, density, per_axis)

Equal masses on a centered cubic lattice inside a triaxial ellipsoid.

per_axis lattice nodes span the longest diameter; nodes on or inside the ellipsoid are kept and each carries density * spacing**3. The set is mirror symmetric in all three axes, so it is centered and principal. Its mass and moments are those of the lattice, not of the continuous ellipsoid; refine per_axis to approach the latter.

MasconSet.distribution

distribution(self, order=6, *, reference_radius=None, tolerance=1e-12)

MomentDistribution holding these exact moments through order.

First moments and products of inertia must vanish to tolerance relative to M L^degree (L the bounding radius); within it they are recorded as the exact zeros the centered principal-axis backends need. A body that is not centered and principal is rejected, never rotated.
