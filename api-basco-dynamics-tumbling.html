basco.dynamics.tumbling

The nominal motion of the binary by itself, and how the secondary is oriented along it.

trajectory = propagate_nominal(model, duration=..., dt=60.0, cadence=600.0)
attitude = attitude_history(trajectory)      # roll, pitch, yaw of the secondary

No partials and no covariance here: only the GUBAS state over time. The angles describe the secondary relative to the orbit frame (called the Hill or RTN frame), which is how libration and tumbling are usually shown.

EulerAngleRange

EulerAngleRange(start_s: float, stop_s: float, sample_count: int, minimum_rad: object, maximum_rad: object, half_range_rad: object, full_turn: object)

The range of roll, pitch and yaw over one stretch of time without a singularity.

start_s, stop_s, sample_count: the stretch. minimum_rad, maximum_rad: (3,) smallest and largest sampled angle. half_range_rad: half of maximum minus minimum. For a libration this is its amplitude, read off the samples and not fitted. full_turn: (3,) True when an angle covers 2 pi or more within the stretch.

Euler angles are singular at a pitch of 90 degrees, and the angles before and after such a point cannot be compared, so ranges are given per stretch between them.

AttitudeHistory(epochs: object, angles_rad: object, unwrapped_angles_rad: object, near_gimbal: object, euler_valid: object, unwrap_segment: object, body_axes_hill: object, spin_body_rad_s: object, spin_tilt_rad: object, excursions: tuple, diagnostics: dict)

The orientation of the secondary relative to the orbit frame, over time.

The columns of the angle arrays are roll, pitch and yaw [rad].

epochs: (n,) [s]. angles_rad: (n, 3) angles, roll and yaw within [-pi, pi]. unwrapped_angles_rad: (n, 3) the same without the jumps of 2 pi, or None. near_gimbal: (n,) True close to the singularity at a pitch of 90 degrees. euler_valid: (n,) False where the angles are unusable because of it. unwrap_segment: (n,) number of the singularity-free stretch each sample is in. body_axes_hill: (n, 3, 3) the secondary's axes as columns, in the orbit frame; a description of the attitude that has no singularity. spin_body_rad_s: (n, 3) inertial spin vector of the secondary, in its own frame. spin_tilt_rad: (n,) angle between that spin vector and the secondary's z axis. excursions: one EulerAngleRange per stretch. diagnostics: settings and checks.

propagate_nominal

propagate_nominal(model, duration, dt=10.0, cadence=60.0, *, dcm_tolerance=1e-05)

Nominal-only coupled Hou propagation through the retained C++ RK4 value integrator. It requests no STM, parameter sensitivities, observations, priors or covariance solve. Duration/output cadence and cadence/step must be compatible integer grids. Native30 states retain their original units/rotation drift; output domain checks are sampled checks, not continuous contact detection.

Propagate the binary alone with the legacy C++ RK4 integrator; returns a NominalTrajectory.

duration, dt, cadence: seconds (or astropy times). dt must divide cadence, which must divide duration. The first and the last time are both returned.

The attitude matrices are not re-orthogonalized along the way. The check that the two Brillouin spheres stay apart is made at the saved times only.

attitude_history

attitude_history(history, *, sequence='XYZ', unwrap=True, window=None, gimbal_tolerance=1e-06, dcm_tolerance=1e-05, project_rotations=True, max_angle_step=np.pi / 2)

Data-only Hill-relative attitude diagnostics. Choose intrinsic XYZ (123) or ZYX (321), with output columns always roll/pitch/yaw. Local unwrapping splits at singular/unresolved chart changes. Excursions are sampled half-ranges, not fitted periodic amplitudes or dynamical classifications. Body directions remain useful when Euler angles are unavailable.

Roll, pitch and yaw of the secondary relative to the orbit frame, as an AttitudeHistory.

The orbit frame H has x from the primary to the secondary, z along the orbit's angular momentum r x v, and y = z cross x. With C_NB = C_NA C_AB the attitude of the secondary in inertial axes and C_NH the orbit frame, the attitude relative to the orbit is C_HB = C_NH^T C_NB.

sequence: "XYZ": C_HB = Rx(roll) Ry(pitch) Rz(yaw). "ZYX": C_HB = Rz(yaw) Ry(pitch) Rx(roll). Both return the columns roll, pitch, yaw. project_rotations: a propagated attitude matrix is slightly off a perfect rotation. By default the nearest rotation is used for these angles (the states themselves are untouched) and the size of the correction is reported. With False, a drift above 1e-12 is an error. max_angle_step: removing the jumps of 2 pi from roll and yaw is not attempted across a singularity, nor across a step larger than this between two samples. window: (start_s, stop_s) limits the samples used for the ranges.

The output cadence must resolve the motion: a full turn between two samples cannot be seen.
