BASCO documentation

BASCO does two things for a binary asteroid such as Didymos and Dimorphos. Particle simulation propagates any number of massless particles around the rotating binary and shows where they impact. Covariance analysis computes how well a set of observations would determine the two masses and gravity fields, and how that uncertainty carries over to a particle's trajectory.

The two bodies move under the full two-body equations with Hou's mutual gravity. The partial derivatives come from automatic differentiation by default; hand-derived analytic partials are an explicit choice.

Results and scope

The Hera paper's analysis uses gravity through order 6 and estimates inertia integrals through degree 4; the recorded validation reproduces all 36 printed values of its Table 2. Consider parameters and covariance histories compares fixed, considered and estimated higher-degree fields. Particle trajectories covers the gravity models, solar forces and outcomes of the particle simulation. Particle and impact covariance propagates the uncertainty of a massless particle under both rotating bodies. Local-field gravity represents the gravity near a patch of surface and its uncertainty. 3D approach and impact, surface close-ups and long approaches and flybys show trajectories with their position uncertainty as figures, videos and interactive views.

The covariance results are formal uncertainties from a linearized analysis, not a fit to mission data. Fitting actual observations, sequential filters and complete end-to-end finite-difference validation remain open work. See capabilities and limits and the validation records.

Many figures and validation records in this manual were made before the code was reorganized in November 2026. Their numbers are unchanged; file and option names in those records are the ones of their time.
