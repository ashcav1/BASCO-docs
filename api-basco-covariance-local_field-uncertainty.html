basco.covariance.local_field.uncertainty

The covariance of the coefficients of a local field.

The coefficients b depend on the uncertain inputs q, b = b0 + J dq, and may have further uncertainty of their own. CoefficientCovariance keeps both, so that quantities depending on q and on b together (such as a trajectory through the local field) get the right correlations.

A combination of coefficients can also be declared unknown. Quantities that depend on it then get no sigma, instead of a made-up one.

CoefficientCovariance

Import as basco.covariance.local_field.CoefficientCovariance

CoefficientCovariance(layout: ParameterLayout, coefficients: object, initial_covariance: object = field(repr=False), jacobian: object, additional_factor: object, unresolved_scaled: object, conventions: dict, _source_latent_count: int = field(repr=False))

Construct with direct or CoefficientMap.bind. The object maps coefficient outputs through one original joint initial_covariance, preserving state/mass/gravity cross terms, consider cancellation and null support. Finite added independent errors and genuinely unresolved modes are separate. No simulated observations, covariance update or new parameter fit is performed.

Coefficients of a local field with their covariance.

layout, coefficients: names and nominal values of the coefficients. initial_covariance: the InitialCovariance of the uncertain inputs q. jacobian: J = d coefficients / d q. additional_factor: a factor of further, independent coefficient uncertainty. unresolved_scaled: combinations of coefficients declared unknown.

CoefficientCovariance.direct

direct(cls, layout, coefficients, initial_covariance, coefficient_jacobian=None, *, additional_factor=None, unresolved_scaled=None, conventions=None)

Accept a declared local nominal vector and full named source without an exterior model or archive. Matching coefficient labels/units select source rows by default; an explicit physical Jacobian allows other source definitions. Shared latent columns retain arbitrary state–mass–coefficient correlations. Declared finite singular distributions are valid; unknown modes must be separately marked unresolved_scaled.

Coefficients and their covariance given directly, without fitting to another model.

CoefficientCovariance.joint_report

joint_report(self, labels, values, units, source_jacobian, *, coefficient_jacobian=None, mapped_source_factor=None, conventions=None)

Without coefficient_jacobian, concatenate source-only outputs and all coefficients, retaining their cross blocks. With H=coefficient_jacobian, report coupled outputs using source_jacobian+H@jacobian before the original factor map. This retains correlated position/gravity effects and consider cancellation. An optional transported source factor must describe that complete output map; full physical Jacobians still decide support.

Quantities that depend on q, reported together with all coefficients, with the correlations between the two.
