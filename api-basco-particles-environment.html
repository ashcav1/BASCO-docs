basco.particles.environment

The environment a particle moves in: the binary, its two shape models and their gravity.

environment = didymos_environment(state="AC2")    # Didymos and Dimorphos, ready to use
site = environment.site("Dimorphos", latitude_deg=0.0, longitude_deg=180.0)

A body is named by its name ("Dimorphos"), or by 0 for the primary and 1 for the secondary.

Environment

Import as basco.particles.Environment

Environment(binary, shapes, gravity='polyhedral', backend='ad', sun=None, shadow='none')

The binary, its two shape models and the gravity model acting on particles.

binary: BinaryModel: the two bodies, their initial state and mutual gravity order. shapes: the two shape models (TriangleMesh), each in its body-fixed frame, in metres. gravity: the gravity model of each body acting on a particle: "polyhedral" the constant-density polyhedron of the shape model; valid everywhere outside the surface "hou" the series used for the binary; valid outside the body's Brillouin sphere only "hou_no_brillouin_check" the same series evaluated inside that sphere too, where it is not guaranteed to be accurate; only for comparing "point" a point mass backend: "ad" or "analytic": how the binary's own equations of motion are evaluated. sun: optional basco.particles.SolarForces (solar gravity and radiation pressure). shadow: with a sun: "none", "sphere" or "mesh" eclipses by the two bodies.

Environment.inertial_states

inertial_states(self, initial_conditions, site=None, body=None)

Inertial states (N, 6) [m, m/s] of an InitialConditions table at the initial time.

With site, the table is in that site's frame (x east, y north, z up, measured from the site point). With body, it is in that body's frame. In both cases the velocity is relative to the rotating body, so the body's own motion and spin are added here. With neither, the table is already inertial.

Environment.effective_gravity

effective_gravity(self, site, height_m=0.05)

Net downward acceleration [m/s^2] felt just above a site, in the body's frame.

It adds the body's own gravity, the centrifugal term of its spin and the tide of the other body. A negative value means the surface cannot hold a resting particle.

didymos_environment

Import as basco.particles.didymos_environment

didymos_environment(state='AC2', gravity='polyhedral', order=6, backend='ad', shapes=None, sun=None, shadow='none')

Didymos and Dimorphos with their shape models.

state: "AC0", "AC1", "AC2" or "AC3": the Hera paper's initial states (see basco.binary.didymos). gravity: gravity model for particles (see Environment). order: order of the Hou series for the binary's own mutual gravity. shapes: folder with didymos.obj and dimorphos.obj in metres; data/shapes by default.

The masses and the initial state are the paper's. The bodies' mass moments are computed from the shape models, so the binary's mutual gravity and the particle gravity describe the same two objects. The initial orbit is not retuned for these shapes.
