basco.gravity.fields

Gravity of one body on a point, in three simple forms.

Each field gives the acceleration [m/s^2] at points given in the body's own frame [m]:

field.value(point)     one point (3,)       -> (3,)
field.values(points)   many points (N, 3)   -> (N, 3)

PolyhedralField (in polyhedral.py) offers the same two calls and is valid down to the surface. The fields here are a point mass and the Hou series.

HouParticleField

Import as basco.particles.HouParticleField

HouParticleField(body, *, order=6, G=G_SI, backend='ad')

The Hou series of the body, valid outside its Brillouin sphere.

The series is the body's exterior multipole expansion. It only converges outside the Brillouin sphere: the smallest sphere about the centre of mass that encloses the whole body (radius brillouin_radius). Asking for a point inside that sphere raises FieldDomainError, and the propagator ends a particle that reaches it with "domain_exit".

HouFieldWithoutBrillouinCheck

Import as basco.particles.HouFieldWithoutBrillouinCheck

HouFieldWithoutBrillouinCheck(body, *, order=6, G=G_SI, backend='ad')

The Hou series used everywhere, also inside the Brillouin sphere. For experiments.

A truncated series is a finite polynomial in 1/r, so it returns numbers inside the Brillouin sphere too. Nothing guarantees those numbers approximate the gravity there, and raising the order does not have to improve them. Use this only to measure that error against a field that is valid there, such as PolyhedralField, or to reproduce work that omits the check (the public GUBAS SRF4BP code does). The propagator has no "domain_exit" for this field, so give it surfaces to impact. brillouin_radius is the radius outside which the series does converge.
