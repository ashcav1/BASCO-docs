basco.kaula

Reusable shape-derived Kaula laws and covariance prior producers.

The paper recipe fits coefficient magnitudes, without subtracting nominal coefficients. Separate zonal/nonzonal fits use RMS across perturbed shapes; the adopted amplitude clears every shape and interior member. Fit degrees, applied degrees, geometry, and estimated coordinates are independent choices.

Import as basco.build_shape_ensemble

build_shape_ensemble(mesh, sampler, *, reference_radius_m, body, max_degree=12, density_models=(), realign=False)

Generate coefficient statistics from shape and optional density members, independently of dynamical order. realign=True aligns each perturbed copy; False retains its perturbation-frame convention. These are scientifically different ensembles.

Compute fresh coefficients from caller-chosen shape/density generators.

Degree12 on high-resolution meshes is deliberately explicit and expensive. There is no runtime dependency on PyGUBAS paths or hidden archived rows. realign=False preserves the paper's perturbation-frame convention; choose True for newly centred principal-frame copies in another scientific study.

KaulaPrior

Import as basco.KaulaPrior

KaulaPrior(parameters: object, body_rules: dict, raw_moment_priors: bool = True, name: str = 'Shape-derived Kaula prior')

Map the rule normalization/radius through the physical-coordinate Jacobian. raw_moment_priors=True additionally selects the paper's independent RMS-touching raw-moment constraints; it is separate from harmonic knowledge and adds no implicit mass/state/barycenter prior.

Generic normalized-harmonic priors plus optional paper raw-moment rows.

body_rules maps A/B (or primary/secondary) to KaulaRule. The additional independent raw rows follow paper Eq13's RMS of touching coefficient sigmas. They are a separate modeling choice, not an inverse harmonic transform. No mass, state, or nuisance prior is introduced.
