ADEvaluator

Import as basco.ad.ADEvaluator

ADEvaluator(model, parameters)

Copied equations of motion and their AD Jacobians at one state.

Returns native F, A = dF/d(native30) and B = dF/d(raw slot) per native unit (kg km^(l+m+n)) in parameters.raw_slots order, exactly what integration.integrate_variational expects. Masses, principal moments and the reduced mass are rebuilt from the cubes inside the differentiated function, so every route of a moment into F appears in B.
