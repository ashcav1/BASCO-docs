basco.covariance.noise

Observation noise, and whitening.

Least squares assumes observations with independent errors of unit variance. Real noise has other sigmas and can be correlated, so each row of the design matrix H (and each residual) is first whitened: divided by its sigma, or for a noise covariance R = L L^T multiplied by L^-1. After whitening, the information matrix is simply H^T H.

Import as basco.covariance.CorrelatedNoise

CorrelatedNoise(covariance: object)

A positive-definite full residual covariance, in the exact row order and mixed row units of the linearization. Cholesky whitening retains correlations. Large dense matrices may be expensive.

Correlated noise with covariance matrix R (symmetric, positive definite).

R = L L^T (Cholesky) is factored once. Whitening solves L W = rows, that is W = L^-1 rows.
