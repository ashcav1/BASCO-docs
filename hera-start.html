Run and customize the Hera analysis

The Hera covariance paper asks how well Hera's observations would determine the masses and gravity fields of Didymos and Dimorphos. BASCO has that analysis as short scripts in examples/covariance/hera/. Each has its settings at the top and runs from top to bottom.

Run one paper case

source tools/activate
python examples/covariance/hera/one_case.py

one_case.py is the whole analysis:

import basco.covariance as bc
from basco.covariance.hera import paper as hera
from basco.covariance.hera.analysis import paper_analysis

CASE = "AC2"   # AC0 equilibrium, AC1 libration, AC2 limited tumbling, AC3 full tumbling
ORBITS = 16    # mutual orbits observed; the paper uses 16, and 1 is enough for a quick look
DT_S = 75.0    # RK4 step

analysis = paper_analysis(CASE, duration=ORBITS * hera.PERIOD_S, dt=DT_S)
design = analysis.design_matrix()   # integrates the variational equations
result = design.covariance()

report = hera.hera_report(result)   # the quantities of the paper, with their 1-sigma
print(report.summary())
hera.print_table2({CASE: hera.table2_percent(result)})

Sixteen orbits take about 25 minutes. ORBITS = 1 takes under two minutes and is enough to see that everything works; its uncertainties are larger than the paper's.

The setup is the paper's: the relative position, the attitude of both bodies and the position of Didymos about the barycentre are observed every 15 minutes; both masses, the initial state, the barycentre's position and velocity, and the inertia integrals of degrees 2 to 4 of both bodies are estimated; the a priori information is the paper's Kaula rule. The Hera results lists the numbers and compares them with the paper's Table 2.

paper_case.py is the same for the full-tumbling case, saved with all report tables and the standard figures.

Choose a rotation state

CASE selects one of the paper's four initial rotation states of Dimorphos:

Case Rotation of Dimorphos
AC0 Equilibrium: circular, planar, synchronous
AC1 Libration only
AC2 Limited tumbling
AC3 Full tumbling

all_cases.py runs the four one after the other and prints Table 2 for all of them. tools/evidence/hera_all_cases.py does the same in parallel processes and takes options (--orbits, --jobs, --figures).

Change masses, shapes and the initial orbit

custom_system.py keeps the paper's observations, estimated parameters and a priori information, and lets you set the binary:

from basco.covariance.hera import paper as hera
from basco.covariance.hera.analysis import HERA_PAPER_DATA, hera_like_binary, make_analysis

model = hera_like_binary(
    primary_mass_kg=hera.MASS_A_KG,
    secondary_mass_kg=hera.MASS_B_KG,
    secondary_semi_axes_m=(96.0, 74.0, 59.0),
    secondary_to_primary_euler_xyz_deg=(0.0, 3.0, 15.0),   # orientation of the secondary
    secondary_spin_period_h=11.921493,
    secondary_spin_tilt_deg=30.0,   # angle of the spin vector from the secondary's +z axis
    position_m=None,                # secondary minus primary in primary axes; None keeps AC2
    velocity_m_s=None,
    gravity_degree=6,
)
analysis = make_analysis(HERA_PAPER_DATA, model=model, gravity_degree=6, dt=75.0)

The primary keeps the shape of the paper's Didymos, with its inertia integrals scaled to the new mass. The secondary is a uniform ellipsoid with the given semi-axes. Changing the masses does not adjust the orbit: the relative position and velocity stay those of case AC2 unless you give them.

For a binary that is not Didymos at all, build the BinaryModel yourself as in Installation and first scripts and pass it as model=.

Change the covariance assumptions

make_analysis (and paper_analysis, which calls it) takes these choices:

Argument Meaning Paper
gravity_degree Order of the mutual gravity: 2, 4 or 6 6
estimate_degrees Degrees whose gravity parameters are estimated (2, 3, 4)
parameterization "raw": inertia integrals; "harmonics": harmonics above degree 2 "raw"
higher_degree_bodies Whose degrees above 2 are estimated: "both", "primary", "secondary" "both"
prior "kaula", "none", or a list of your own a priori objects "kaula"
dt RK4 step [s] 75
duration Observation span [s] 16 orbits
partials "ad" or "analytic" "ad"

The other examples in the folder each vary one of them:

Script What it varies
degree_trades.py Which degrees are estimated and whether the Kaula rule is used, all from one design matrix
consider_and_histories.py Higher degrees estimated, considered or fixed, and the covariance as observations accumulate
multi_arc.py Independent arcs and a daily gap in the observations
kaula_priors.py The paper's Kaula rules fitted again from its shape ensembles
kaula_from_a_shape.py A Kaula rule for a shape of your own

Changing noise, a priori information or the set of estimated parameters does not need a new propagation; see Reuse and trades. Changing the binary, the order of the gravity or the step size does.

To change the observations themselves, build the CovarianceAnalysis yourself: make_analysis in src/basco/covariance/hera/analysis.py is short enough to copy from.
