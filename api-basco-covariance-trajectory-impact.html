basco.covariance.trajectory.impact

Propagation to an impact, and the covariance of the impact time and point.

integrate_to_impact integrates the variational equations with fixed RK4 steps until an event function (the signed distance to a surface) reaches zero from outside. Steps are shortened as the surface approaches, and the crossing is then located inside the last step.

impact_covariance turns the covariance at the impact time into the covariance of the impact itself. At a fixed time, the position p has partials J with respect to the uncertain inputs q. A change of q also changes when the particle reaches the surface. With n the outward normal and v the particle's velocity relative to the surface, staying on the surface requires n . (J dq + v dt) = 0, so

d t_impact / dq = -(n . J) / (n . v)
d p_impact / dq = J + v (d t_impact / dq)

This is first-order and needs a clear crossing: it is refused for a grazing impact, where n . v is close to zero.

integrate_to_impact

Import as basco.covariance.trajectory.integrate_to_impact

integrate_to_impact(evaluate, initial, n_parameters, event, duration, dt, cadence, *, distance_tolerance_m=1e-08, time_tolerance_s=1e-07, domain_errors=(), max_steps=100000, normal_speed_tolerance=1e-10, relative_grazing_tolerance=1e-07)

Use the shared augmented RK4 step for state, STM and parameter sensitivities. Event value is a positive-outside distance and event.rate is exact normal speed. Inward gap/rate limiting and one-sided residual/time criteria avoid silently extending a force below its accepted domain. Explicit outcomes include contact, no_impact within horizon, domain_exit, grazing, edge, initial_inside/contact and numerical_failure. Chronological resolved steps are not an unconditional arbitrary-root certificate.

Integrate state, Phi and Psi until event reports an impact.

evaluate: returns (F, A, B) at a state. event: gives the signed distance to the surface at a state, and its rate. duration, dt, cadence: [s]. The propagation stops at duration without an impact. distance_tolerance_m, time_tolerance_s: how closely the crossing is located.

No impact within duration does not prove that the particle escapes.

impact_covariance

Import as basco.covariance.trajectory.impact_covariance

impact_covariance(initial_covariance, position, body_velocity, normal, position_jacobian, position_factor, *, epoch_s, conventions=None, normal_speed_tolerance=1e-10, relative_grazing_tolerance=1e-07)

Correct fixed-time target-body position derivatives for uncertain event time: t_q=-n*J/(n*v), J_contact=J+v*t_q. Apply the same operation to original transported latent columns, retaining full-J source support and consider/null conventions. Requires a smooth inward event and nonzero normal speed; no covariance is manufactured for grazing or unavailable directions.

The ImpactCovariance from quantities at the nominal impact time (the equations at the top of this file).

position, body_velocity, normal: the impact point [m], the velocity relative to the surface [m/s] and the outward unit normal, in the body's frame. position_jacobian: J = d position / d q at fixed time. position_factor: J F, the same partials applied to the covariance factor. epoch_s: the nominal impact time.
