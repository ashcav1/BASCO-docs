basco.ad.conservation

Energy and angular momentum of the full two-body problem, for any gravity model.

Both are conserved by the continuous unperturbed dynamics whatever the mutual potential, so their drift along a propagated trajectory tests a gravity model and its equations of motion together. Native state layout (state.py):

E = m |v|^2 / 2 + w_A . I_A w_A / 2 + w_B . I_B w_B / 2 + U(r, C_AB)
H_N = C_NA (m r x v + I_A w_A + C_AB I_B C_AB^T w_B)

with v the inertial relative velocity and both spin rates resolved in the primary frame, w_B = C_AB^T (omega_B in A) in the secondary frame, I the principal moments and m the reduced mass, all built from the model's moments. Results are SI: joules and kg m^2/s.
