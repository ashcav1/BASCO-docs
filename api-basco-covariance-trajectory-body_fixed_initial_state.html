basco.covariance.trajectory.body_fixed_initial_state

The initial state of a particle given relative to one body, and its inertial equivalent with partials.

For a body with centre c, velocity v_body, rotation matrix C and rotation rate dC/dt, a point p moving at u in the body's frame has the inertial state

r = c + C p        v = v_body + dC/dt p + C u

Because c, C and so on come from the binary's state, an error in the binary's state or masses shifts the particle's inertial initial state. BodyFixedInitialState.evaluate returns r and v with their partials with respect to the GUBAS state, the two masses, and p and u themselves.

BodyFixedInitialState

Import as basco.covariance.trajectory.BodyFixedInitialState

BodyFixedInitialState(body: str, position: object, velocity: object)

COM-relative offset rho and its body-frame time derivative u on exactly primary or secondary. The inertial map is r=c+R rho, v=vc+Rdot rho+R u. Rotation rates use the coded binary equations at the retained state, without DCM projection or an off-manifold orthogonality substitution. This map does not assert contact with a surface.

An initial position and velocity in one body's fixed frame.

body: "A" (primary) or "B" (secondary). position: (3,) p [m] from the body's centre of mass. velocity: (3,) u [m/s] relative to the rotating body.
