BASCO computes formal covariance predictions for binary-asteroid masses, gravity parameters and initial states using coupled translational and rotational dynamics.

A study specifies the bodies and nominal state, mutual gravity order, estimated parameters, observations, measurement noise and priors. The maintained gravity model is the Hou full two-body expansion. Automatic differentiation is the default derivative method; native analytic derivatives are also available.

Validation and scope

Consider parameters and covariance histories compares fixed, uncertain and estimated higher-degree fields in a Hera example, with mass and orbit-uncertainty plots. The CubeSat landing roadmap identifies the additional dynamics and contact sensitivities needed for a surface footprint.

The Hera reproduction uses gravity through order 6 and estimated moments through degree 4. The recorded validation reproduces all 36 printed Table 2 values. Results and comparison criteria and validation records describe the evidence.

These results are formal covariance predictions. Generic batch-estimation interfaces are available; complete binary observation-data fitting, sequential filters and complete end-to-end finite-difference validation remain open work. See capabilities and limits.
