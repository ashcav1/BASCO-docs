Particle

Import as basco.particles.Particle

Particle(name, position, velocity, area_to_mass=0.0, reflectivity=1.0)

A named point particle at the start of an integration.

position: (3,) inertial position [m], measured from the binary's barycentre. velocity: (3,) inertial velocity [m/s]. area_to_mass: area facing the Sun divided by mass [m^2/kg]; only used with solar radiation pressure. reflectivity: multiplies the radiation pressure (1 absorbs everything).

Plain numbers are taken as SI; astropy quantities are converted. The particle has no mass of its own: it does not pull on the bodies or on other particles.

ParticleOutcome

Import as basco.particles.ParticleOutcome

ParticleOutcome(kind, epoch_s, state, body=None, boundary_distance_m=None, boundary_rate_m_s=None)

How and when one particle ended.

kind: "completed" (no terminal event by the final time), "impact", "domain_exit" or "grazing"; see propagator.py. epoch_s: the time it ended [s]. state: (6,) its last inertial position [m] and velocity [m/s]. body: "A" (primary) or "B" (secondary) where it ended; None when completed. boundary_distance_m, boundary_rate_m_s: its signed distance to that boundary and the rate of change of that distance when it was terminated; None when completed.

StateHistory

Import as basco.particles.StateHistory

StateHistory(epochs, binary_states, particle_states, particles, outcomes, model, metadata)

The states saved by one propagation of the binary with its particles.

epochs: (T,) the saved times [s]. binary_states: (T, 30) the GUBAS state of the binary at those times (km, km/s, rad/s and attitude matrices; see basco/binary/state.py). particle_states: (T, P, 6) inertial position [m] and velocity [m/s] of each particle. Rows after a particle's terminal event are NaN; its last real row is at the time in its outcome. particles: the Particle inputs, in the order of the arrays. outcomes: one ParticleOutcome per particle. model: the BinaryModel that was integrated. metadata: the settings used and a few counts (steps, evaluations, seconds).
