basco.covariance.trajectory.dynamics

Equations of motion of the binary with one particle, and their partials.

This is the restricted full three-body problem: two extended bodies that move under their mutual gravity, and a particle without mass that feels both. The state has 36 numbers: the GUBAS state of the binary (30) followed by the particle's inertial position [m] and velocity [m/s].

RF3BPDynamics(state) returns F (the time derivative), A = dF/d state and B = dF/d p, where p are the inertia integrals of the two bodies, followed by the coefficients of any local gravity field. The conversion from inertia integrals to the estimated parameters (d T / d parameter) is applied later, once.

The particle simulation uses basco/particles/dynamics.py instead: the same forces for many particles, without A and B.

RF3BPDynamics

Import as basco.covariance.trajectory.RF3BPDynamics

RF3BPDynamics(model, parameters, *, backend='ad', field_order=None, local_fields=None)

Composes the retained Hou binary with two asteroid-to-particle fields. Native30 binary and SI6 particle are integrated at common stages. AD is default; backend='native' selects hand-derived potential/binary partials. No observations or estimator are constructed.

The equations of motion of the binary and one particle, with partials.

model, parameters: the BinaryModel and its EstimatedParameters or ParameterSet. backend: "ad" (default) or "analytic": where the partials come from. field_order: order of the Hou series for the particle's gravity (2, 4 or 6, at most the order of the mutual gravity, which is the default). local_fields: {"A": field} and/or {"B": field} replaces one body's gravity on the particle by a local field model (see basco/covariance/local_field), which is valid closer to the surface. The binary's own motion is not affected.

The Hou series is evaluated only outside each body's Brillouin sphere; a particle inside one raises FieldDomainError.
