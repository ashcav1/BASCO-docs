basco.covariance

Covariance analysis of a binary asteroid's masses and gravity field.

import basco.covariance as bc

analysis = bc.CovarianceAnalysis(model, parameters, observations, bc.RK4(60.0))
design = analysis.design_matrix()
result = design.covariance()

Where things are (docs/user_guide/COVARIANCE_ANALYSIS.md walks through them):

analysis.py           CovarianceAnalysis: the inputs, and the steps in order
observations.py       what is observed and when; schedules.py, noise.py, nuisance.py
design_matrix.py      the rows of partials of the observations
priors.py, kaula.py   a priori information
solvers.py            from rows to a covariance; partitioned_solver.py
results.py            the result and its reports; results_tables.py, exports.py
consider.py           parameters that are uncertain but not estimated
history.py            the covariance as observations accumulate
multi_arc.py          several arcs with shared parameters; multi_arc_analysis.py
hera/                 the Hera paper's analysis
trajectory/           the covariance of a particle's trajectory
local_field/          a local gravity field near the surface, and its covariance
plots/                figures

The binary itself is in basco/binary and the variational equations in basco/dynamics.
