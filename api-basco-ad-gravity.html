basco.ad.gravity

BASCO 0.1.0Hou AD default · native availableSource c00d5ff

JAX mutual potentials: the Hou copy, an SI adapter and a closed-form check.

Every model here returns only the mutual potential energy U. The copied equations of motion obtain forces and torques from jax.grad(U), and ADTransport differentiates those equations again for A and B. A new gravity model therefore needs U and nothing else.

Native (copied) units are km, kg, s; the SI adapter converts at its boundary.

SIPotential

Import as basco.ad.SIPotential

SIPotential(gravity: object, order: int)

Adapt a user gravity model written in SI to the copied native units.

gravity.potential(r, C_AB, T_A, T_B, G) receives the secondary's center of mass relative to the primary's, in metres resolved in the primary frame A; C_AB mapping secondary-frame components into A; mass-weighted moment cubes T[l, m, n] = int x^l y^m z^n dm in kg m^(l+m+n), each in its own body frame; and G in m^3/(kg s^2). It returns the mutual potential energy in joules and must be JAX-traceable. Unit conversions happen here, inside the differentiated function.

SecondDegreeGravity

Import as basco.ad.SecondDegreeGravity

SecondDegreeGravity(mutual_order: int = 2)

Closed-form mutual potential to second degree (MacCullagh), written in SI.

U = -G [ M_A M_B / R + (M_A (3 e.S_B e - tr S_B) + M_B (3 e.S_A e - tr S_A)) / (2 R^3) ]

with second-moment matrices S = [int x_i x_j dm], S_B rotated into A by C_AB, and e = r/R. For centered bodies this equals the Hou expansion at total mutual order 2. It is written independently of the Hou series and is the smallest example of replacing the gravity model under AD.
