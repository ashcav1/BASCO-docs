basco.covariance.trajectory.impact_trajectory

A particle trajectory to its impact on a body, with the covariance along the way and at the impact.

trajectory = propagate_to_impact(dynamics, initial, initial_covariance, target="B",
                                 surface=surface, duration=..., local_uncertainties=())
trajectory.impact.report.summary()

The uncertain inputs q are those of the InitialCovariance, plus the coefficients of a local gravity field when one is used (local_uncertainties).

ImpactTrajectory(integration: object, initial_covariance: object, initialization: object, force_source_jacobian: object, event: object, position_history: object, impact: object, diagnostics: object, initial_source_jacobian: object = None)

Own the common-stage approach transport, original source/initialization, force-coordinate map, fixed-time target-body position history and separate event-corrected contact report. A no-contact finite forecast, invalid field domain or nonsmooth event remains explicit. export writes standard atomic numeric artifacts, not a new estimator result.

A trajectory to impact with its covariance.

integration: the ImpactVariationalSolution. initial_covariance: the InitialCovariance used. initialization: the ParticleInitialState. force_source_jacobian: d(force parameters) / d q. initial_source_jacobian: J0 = d(initial state) / d q. event: the SurfaceImpactEvent. position_history: covariance of the position in the target body's frame over time. impact: the ImpactCovariance, or None without an impact.

inertial_history(self, *, quantity='position')

Map the retained original source into barycentric inertial position, velocity or six-state covariance with Cartesian SI values. Reuses the trajectory's stored initial/force source maps, state transition and force sensitivities; preserves original factor, null/consider and cross-covariance semantics. Changes neither the trajectory nor the information cutoff. Body-relative covariance remains a separate output.

The covariance of the inertial position, velocity or both ("state") over time.

propagate_to_impact

Import as basco.covariance.trajectory.propagate_to_impact

propagate_to_impact(dynamics, initial, initial_covariance, *, local_uncertainties, target, surface, duration, cadence=1.0, integrator=None, epoch_mapping=None, distance_tolerance_m=1e-08, time_tolerance_s=1e-07, ignored_labels=())

Propagate an explicitly selected local particle field and full joint uncertainty toward a fixed target surface. The binary retains Hou dynamics and the companion retains its selected field. Raw moment/local coefficient maps are composed once into the original source; transported latent columns preserve cross terms and consider cancellation. No automatic field switch or additional monopole is added.

Propagate a particle to its impact on body target and return an ImpactTrajectory.

dynamics: RF3BPDynamics. initial: ParticleState or BodyFixedInitialState. initial_covariance: the InitialCovariance. local_uncertainties: uncertainties of local gravity fields, or () for none. target, surface: "A" or "B", and that body's surface. duration, cadence: [s].
