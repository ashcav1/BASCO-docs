basco.covariance.plots.closeups

Close views, at the scale of metres, of a particle's position uncertainty near a surface.

Two views of a TrajectoryScene: the uncertainty ellipsoid above the surface in 3-D, and its projection on the surface facet where the impact happens. The projection shows where the particle is likely to be at each time, seen along the facet's normal. The impact ellipse, which accounts for the uncertain impact time, is drawn as a fixed reference.

CloseupCamera

CloseupCamera(center_m: object, span_m: float, elevation_deg: float = 25.0, azimuth_deg: float = -55.0)

Fixed physical camera box in the target body's frame. The centre and cubic span use metres, and angles use degrees. This crops display geometry; it does not magnify the ellipsoid or move the physical surface.

A 3-D view: the point it looks at [m], the width of the cube shown [m] and the two viewing angles [degrees].

SurfaceProjectionHistory

SurfaceProjectionHistory(centres_uv_m: object, covariance_uv_m2: object, available: object, point_m: object, tangent_axes: object, face_triangle_m: object)

Current-position marginal projected into one actual smooth mesh facet. This is a fixed-time geometric projection, distinct from event-corrected first-contact covariance. The availability flag requires the whole supported-rank confidence ellipse to remain inside that same facet.

The particle's position and covariance projected on the impact facet, per epoch.

centres_uv_m, covariance_uv_m2: position and covariance in two coordinates in the facet. available: False where the covariance is not determined. point_m, tangent_axes, face_triangle_m: the facet's reference point, its two in-plane axes and its three corners.

Arguments & defaults
ArgumentType / defaultMeaning
centres_uv_mobject
required		N-by-2 projected current-position means in the fixed facet chart, in metres.
covariance_uv_m2object
required		N-by-2-by-2 projected marginal covariance in square metres, retaining the scene's complete source contributions.
availableobject
required		N boolean flags requiring source support and the complete confidence ellipse to lie inside the actual smooth facet.
point_mobject
required		Nominal contact/chart origin, a target-frame three-vector in metres.
tangent_axesobject
required		Orthonormal 3-by-2 chart directions expressed in target-body axes.
face_triangle_mobject
required		The three vertices of the actual supporting mesh facet, shape3-by-3 in target-frame metres.

surface_projection_history(scene)

The SurfaceProjectionHistory of a scene: every position and covariance projected along the normal of the impact facet.
