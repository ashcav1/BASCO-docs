basco.benchmarks.hera

Hera paper benchmark: exact configuration, archived rows, and comparison.

The 2026-10-16 paper's Table 2 comes from round 20, four single-arc raw34ABm cases. Round 21 supplies sensitivity studies, not replacement baseline cases. This module keeps the two sources distinguishable.

The source parameter vector contains mass-normalized SI inertia integrals and GM_A/GM_B, represented by fractional increments. Its local state uses km, km/s, rad/s, and left attitude errors. Six extra local columns represent the barycenter as Legendre P0/P1 coefficients in km. Archived observables are km for both position channels and rad for attitudes.

Replay converts those rows to BASCO physical SI coordinates, including R0 and V0 at t0=0, before invoking the ordinary covariance interface. It is an archived-row replay and does not propagate a trajectory. Fresh propagation uses build_hera_model, hera_parameters, and hera_priors with the ordinary user-selected transport and observation components.

The paper posterior includes BOTH harmonic prior rows and independent raw-moment prior rows ("rows+diag"). Original Kaula sigmas, rather than the combined prior covariance, are the paper's reported harmonic prior. Izz is reported as Izz/(M R**2), a shape quantity, rather than dimensional Izz; this distinction matters when the masses are estimated.

prepare_hera_moments

Import as basco.benchmarks.prepare_hera_moments

prepare_hera_moments(mesh_path)

Prepare complete degree6 SI moments from an explicitly supplied mesh.

This is a value-only preprocessing step, with no trajectory integration. The retained BASCO-owned signed-tetrahedron integration is reused; no module from an original research directory is imported. OBJ coordinates are scaled from km to m, centered, principal-aligned, and pinned to T300>=0/T030>=0. Numerical first moments and products of inertia are explicitly zeroed in the principal-axis representation and recorded.

The returned JSON-compatible object can be saved once and passed to build_hera_model across separate runs. No files are written here.

build_hera_model

Import as basco.benchmarks.build_hera_model

build_hera_model(inputs, state='AC3', *, gravity_degree=6)

Build a Hera model with independent nominal-field and solve-for choices.

gravity_degree selects the total Hou mutual expansion order (2/4/6). The complete degree6 moment tables and the paper's order6-calibrated initial conditions are retained at every order. A gravity-model comparison therefore changes the force/torque model, without silently refitting the initial orbit or changing the nominal mass distribution.

AC0 is circular, planar, synchronous, and uses the source order6 speed calibration 0.995394. AC1/2/3 share e=.0274, primary inclination1deg, secondary obliquity3deg, and pre-impact secondary spin11.921493h. They differ only in initial libration/wobble: (10,0), (10,20), (90,43.09) in degrees. Velocity is inertial velocity resolved in the primary frame.
