Exterior expansions have no general convergence guarantee inside the enclosing sphere. The cylinder is a declared field domain, not automatic shape certification.

Red: no contact within the specified forecast.

Amber: domain exit, grazing or another unresolved outcome.

Probe counts are not a miss probability. An off-surface confidence region is not by itself evidence of an escaping trajectory.

Local views crop displayed geometry to their metre-scale view box. Position regions are fixed-time marginal covariance. Contact contours use separate event-corrected covariance. A red terminal hull, when present, spans the no-contact probe endpoints; it is not a probability region.
