ParticleDynamics

ParticleDynamics(model, particles, *, fields=None, backend='ad', solar=None, surfaces=None, shadow='none')

Value-only equations for native30 binary plus N barycentric SI6 particles.

binary backend is explicit AD/default or native; no variational quantities or covariance are evaluated. Fields are selected per body, independently of the Hou mutual potential. Solar translation uses the acceleration of the binary origin consistently; optional solar torques are explicit.
