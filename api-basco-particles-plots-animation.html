basco.particles.plots.animation

Animations of propagated trajectories.

animate(trajectories, "movie.mp4", frame="Dimorphos")                in Dimorphos's fixed frame
animate(trajectories, "movie.mp4", frame="Dimorphos", view="site")   close-up around the site
animate(trajectories, "movie.mp4", frame="rotating")                 in the rotating binary frame

Each frame shows the bodies, every particle still being propagated as a dot with a fading trail, and every particle that has impacted as a marker that stays on the body it hit. The clock at the top right shows the elapsed time.

animate

Import as basco.particles.animate, basco.particles.plots.animate

animate(trajectories, file, frame=None, view='body', zoom=1.0, fit=0.95, color='v1', color_limits=None, frames=300, fps=24, pacing=1.0, trail=0.06, end_s=None)

Write an animation of the trajectories and return its file (.mp4, or .gif without ffmpeg).

frame: a body's name for its body-fixed frame, "rotating" or "inertial". Default: the body the particles started from, or "rotating". view: in a body-fixed frame, "body" shows the whole body and "site" a close-up of the site. zoom: 2 shows twice as much; "fit" holds the fraction fit of the trajectories. pacing: 1 plays at constant speed. A larger value plays the beginning slowly and the end fast: frame k shows the time end * (k / (frames - 1)) ** pacing. Use about 2 when most particles impact early in a long propagation. trail: length of the fading trail, as a fraction of the animation's time span. end_s: time at the last frame. Default: the full propagation in the rotating frame; in a body-fixed frame, when 98% of the particles have impacted (at least 10 minutes).

impact_marker_positions

impact_marker_positions(trajectories, frame)

A function of time giving the impact point of every impacted particle in frame (NaN for the others).

An impact point is fixed on the body that was hit, so in any frame where that body moves, its marker moves with the body.
