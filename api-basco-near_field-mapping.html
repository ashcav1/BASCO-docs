basco.near_field.mapping

Deterministic local representation changes; collocation adds no information.

The fit changes coordinates for a declared finite source field. It cannot infer interior mass structure or make an exterior truncation accurate below its convergence boundary. Such continuation always requires an explicit assumption.

RepresentationMap

Import as basco.near_field.RepresentationMap

RepresentationMap(coefficients: object, jacobian: object, operator: object, source_layout: CoordinateLayout, coefficient_layout: CoordinateLayout, source_nominal: object, nullspace_scaled: object, completion: str, identity: str, diagnostics: dict, metadata: dict)

Construct with fit, then bind to an original covariance/consider result or SourceUncertainty. Owns the local affine coefficient map, its physical source Jacobian and numerical target nullspace. An explicit conditional completion is recorded because finite exterior information does not uniquely determine a physical near-surface field.

A local affine map b = coefficients + jacobian @ delta_q.

Construct with :meth:fit. operator maps physical collocation samples to coefficients with the same numerical scaling. nullspace_scaled records target modes that those samples do not determine. No residual is converted into a prior or an observation covariance.

RepresentationMap.fit

fit(cls, design, values, source_jacobian, source_layout, coefficient_layout, *, completion, source_nominal=None, row_scales=None, rcond=None, allow_rank_deficient=False, metadata=None)

Use a scaled SVD to fit nominal coefficients and their source derivative from identical deterministic sample rows. No normal matrix, residual-based prior or independent-data covariance is created. Numerical target rank is distinct from source estimation rank. Deficient target fits reject by default; explicitly accepted null directions remain unresolved in bound reports.

Fit a deterministic representation and its physical source Jacobian.

design has sample rows and coefficient columns. values and source_jacobian describe the same source-field samples. Characteristic coefficient/source scales and optional positive row_scales improve numerical conditioning; they supply no statistical weights. completion must describe the physical assumption made outside the known source model's domain. Rank deficiency raises unless explicitly accepted; an accepted nullspace remains unresolved in all bound uncertainty reports.

RepresentationMap.bind

bind(self, source, *, additional_factor=None, unresolved_scaled=None)

Embed named mapping columns into the complete source layout with exact unit checks and known nominal/model-context validation. Retain original map_factor/derived_stable delegation and latent ordering. additional_factor declares independent finite coefficient errors; correlated new errors require a complete joint source through direct(). It does not erase unresolved target directions.

Attach an original null-aware covariance source without refactoring it.

Mapping-source coordinates may be a named subset of the full source. Their units must agree, and known physical nominal values must match. additional_factor declares independent finite coefficient errors; use a complete joint source with :meth:CoefficientUncertainty.direct when new errors correlate with existing coordinates.
