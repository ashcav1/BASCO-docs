basco.plotting.mesh_views

Orthographic, shaded views of body-frame triangle meshes on ordinary 2-D axes.

Plain axes give exact equal scaling, reliable layering of trajectories against the body and clean tick labels, which Matplotlib's 3-D axes cannot guarantee. Faces are culled and depth-sorted for the chosen view and lit by one directional source. Only supplied geometry is drawn; nothing is generated or realigned.

DepthMap

DepthMap(view: OrthographicView, origin: object, pixel_m: float, depth: object)

Nearest-surface depth of triangles in one view, rasterized for fast occlusion tests.

Depth is the coordinate along view.toward, so larger values are nearer the viewer. depth is indexed [row, column] from origin (view metres) in steps of pixel_m and holds -inf where no surface projects. One map serves any number of points, which suits thousands of paths and animation frames where per-point ray casts do not.

DepthMap.from_triangles

from_triangles(cls, view, triangles, *, resolution=1200)

The depth map of triangles (F, 3, 3) seen in view.

The view is divided into pixels (resolution along the longer side). Each triangle is drawn into the pixels it covers, and each pixel keeps the depth of the surface nearest to the viewer. A point is then hidden when the map is nearer the viewer than the point.
