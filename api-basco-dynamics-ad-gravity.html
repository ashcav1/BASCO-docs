basco.dynamics.ad.gravity

The mutual potential energy U of the two bodies, written with JAX.

Only U is written down. The equations of motion get the force and the torques from its derivatives (dynamics.py), and the variational equations differentiate those once more (partials.py). A new gravity model therefore needs U and nothing else.

Hou's series is

U = -G sum over n of (1 / R^(n+1)) u_n(e, T_A, T_B')

with R the distance between the centres of mass, e the unit vector from A to B, T_A the inertia integrals of A and T_B' those of B rotated into the frame of A. u_n collects the products of an integral of A and an integral of B whose degrees add up to n, each with a fixed coefficient and powers of the components of e. The sum stops at n = mutual order. The coefficients are in hou_tables.py.

Units here are those of the GUBAS code: km, kg, s. SIPotential converts for a gravity model that is written in SI units.

SIPotential

Import as basco.dynamics.ad.SIPotential

SIPotential(gravity: object, order: int)

A gravity model written in SI units, made usable where GUBAS units are expected.

Your model provides potential(r, C_AB, T_A, T_B, G): r [m] is the centre of the secondary relative to the primary in the frame of A, T_A and T_B are cubes with T[l, m, n] in kg m^(l+m+n), G is in m^3 / (kg s^2), and the result is in joules. It must be written with jax.numpy so that it can be differentiated. This class converts the units on the way in and on the way out.

SecondDegreeGravity

Import as basco.dynamics.ad.SecondDegreeGravity

SecondDegreeGravity(mutual_order: int = 2)

The mutual potential to second degree in closed form (MacCullagh's formula), in SI.

U = -G [ M_A M_B / R
         + (M_A (3 e.S_B e - tr S_B) + M_B (3 e.S_A e - tr S_A)) / (2 R^3) ]

S is the matrix of second-degree inertia integrals of a body, S_B rotated into the frame of A, and e = r / R. This equals Hou's series at order 2. It is written independently of that series, and is the smallest example of a gravity model of your own.
