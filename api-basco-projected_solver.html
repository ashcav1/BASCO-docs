basco.projected_solver

Householder elimination of local state/nuisance coordinates before rank analysis.

Long chaotic arcs can amplify initial-state columns without similarly amplifying marginal field information. A joint relative cutoff is then a different statistical rank decision from a cutoff on the marginalized field. This optional solver makes that distinction explicit and retains all cross covariance. It never forms or inverts a normal/information matrix.

NuisanceProjectedSolver

Import as basco.NuisanceProjectedSolver

NuisanceProjectedSolver(rank_rtol: object = None, nuisance_condition_limit: float = 100000000000000.0, name: str = 'nuisance-projected Householder QR/SVD')

Alternative Householder elimination of local nuisance directions with marginal physical support diagnostics. Inspect joint versus marginal rank; its support criteria need not produce identical availability decisions to joint scaling.

Rank-test field information after exact QR elimination of local columns.

All local directions must be numerically identifiable from the data block. The explicit condition guard rejects unusable elimination; orthogonal-basis invariance and integration refinement remain independent validation obligations. rank_rtol defaults to sqrt(eps) on the MARGINAL parameter square root. No smaller joint cutoff is selected to manufacture finite uncertainties.
