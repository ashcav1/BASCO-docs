basco.validation.particle_studies.comparison_figures

Figures comparing receiving-field models for the same ejecta, from saved data only.

Colour, line style and marker each encode one quantity across all figures: colour is launch speed in trajectory views and the model in field-error views, markers give the outcome. Actual meshes are drawn at true scale in the body frame, and paths passing behind a body are shown dotted. No force or trajectory is evaluated.
