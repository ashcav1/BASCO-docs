basco.dynamics.fingerprint

The fingerprint of a propagation: a record of everything that determines its result.

Integrating the variational equations is the expensive step of a covariance analysis, so its result is reused whenever possible: for other observation types, noise or a priori information. Reuse is only correct when the binary, the parameters, the output times and the integrator are the same. The fingerprint lists exactly those inputs as text; two propagations may share a result when their fingerprints are equal. Observations, noise and a priori information are not part of it, because they do not change the propagation.

Saved results store the fingerprint, so its wording must not change (see SAVED_TYPE_NAMES).

propagation_fingerprint(model, parameters, schedule, integrator, provider_name)

The fingerprint of one propagation.

It records G, the gravity model, both bodies' inertia integrals and Brillouin radii, the initial state, the estimated parameters with their nominal values and the matrix d T / d parameter, the output times, the integrator, and provider_name: which code computed the partials (the analytic and the AD versions have different names).
