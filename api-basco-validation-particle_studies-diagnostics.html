basco.validation.particle_studies.diagnostics

Data-only inertial osculating diagnostics for saved particle trajectories.

Elements describe the instantaneous two-body conic for the named centre and declared SI GM. They do not classify permanent capture or escape in a binary. The inertial-state requirement and radial/parabolic cautions follow NAIF's oscelt_c / oscltx_c documentation (retrieved 2026-11-09): https://naif.jpl.nasa.gov/pub/naif/toolkit_docs/C/cspice/oscltx_c.html

No ephemeris interpolation, force evaluation or propagation occurs here.

OrbitalElements

OrbitalElements(epochs: object, names: tuple, centre: str, gm: float, semi_major_axis: object, eccentricity: object, periapsis: object, specific_energy: object, angular_momentum: object, near_parabolic: object, valid: object, radial: object)

Saved-clock osculating scalars, shaped (epoch, particle) in SI.

angular_momentum is the specific vector, shaped (epoch,particle,3). valid marks finite, nonzero-radius, nonradial conic states. A valid near-parabolic state retains eccentricity/periapsis/energy/angular momentum but has NaN semimajor axis. Near-parabolic means abs(energy) <= 1e-10 * (v**2/2 + GM/r). Radial states have angular momentum no larger than 1e-12 * r * max(v,sqrt(GM/r)); their conic scalars are NaN, while their finite energy/angular momentum are retained. Missing samples remain NaN and invalid. No angles are inferred at a degenerate orbital plane.

orbital_elements

orbital_elements(trajectory, *, centre='barycenter', gm=None)

Return immutable instantaneous conic diagnostics without changing frame.

Body-relative velocities subtract the body's inertial COM velocity; they never use rotating-frame time derivatives. Default GM is the model's G times the chosen model body mass, or the sum of both masses for barycenter. Override gm explicitly in m^3/s^2 for a different reference conic. Hyperbolic semimajor axes are negative; unresolved parabolic values are NaN. See :class:OrbitalElements for exact masks and numerical thresholds.
