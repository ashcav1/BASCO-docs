basco.covariance.local_field.residual

A local field that is a point mass plus a cylindrical-harmonic remainder.

Most of a body's gravity near its surface is the 1/r^2 pull of its total mass. Taking that part exactly and letting cylindrical harmonics represent only the rest makes the harmonics small, and keeps the dependence on the body's mass explicit.

PointMassPlusLocalField

Import as basco.covariance.local_field.PointMassPlusLocalField

PointMassPlusLocalField(residual: CylindricalHarmonicField, mass: object, G: float = G_SI)

Exact central monopole composed with a cylindrical harmonic residual. The nominal residual must be calibrated after subtracting the same monopole; free residual uncertainty remains separate from mass. The complete field is supported only inside the residual patch, which must exclude the origin. Kernel validation does not certify a new volume or impact fit. Source queries must include both raw-mass and residual-coefficient columns, retaining their cross covariance.

Gravity = point mass at the body's centre + a CylindricalHarmonicField.

residual: the CylindricalHarmonicField. mass: [kg]. G.

The coefficients of the remainder must have been fitted to the field minus the point mass.
